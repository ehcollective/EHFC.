Who We Are

Rooted in indigenous knowledge, guided by innovation

Eastern Himalayas Collective Foundation is a not-for-profit organization committed to advancing soil, society, and sustainability across the Eastern Himalayan landscape of Northeast India. We work with farmers, indigenous communities, women, youth, institutions, and development partners to strengthen livelihoods, conserve natural resources, restore ecosystems, and foster resilient, self-reliant communities.

Rooted in indigenous knowledge and guided by innovation, we promote sustainable agriculture, biodiversity conservation, climate resilience, research, capacity building, and collaborative action to protect the region's rich ecological and cultural heritage while creating lasting environmental, social, and economic impact.

“Inspired by Nature. Empowered by People. Driven by Purpose.”

Mission

To inspire sustainable change by empowering communities, restoring ecosystems, and fostering resilient livelihoods across the Eastern Himalayas.

Vision

A harmonious Eastern Himalayas where healthy soil, vibrant communities, and sustainable choices ensure a thriving planet for generations to come.

"Every meaningful change begins with a shared purpose. We founded this collective to turn that purpose into action for people, nature, and future generations" Founding Team

How We Work

Our Six ‘E’ Framework

Engage

Build meaningful relationships with communities, institutions, and partners through trust, participation, and collaboration.

Explore

Understand local needs, indigenous knowledge, opportunities, and challenges through research, dialogue, and field engagement.

Enrich

Strengthen knowledge, skills, livelihoods, and natural resources through training, innovation, and sustainable practices.

Execute

Implement impactful, community-led initiatives that promote sustainable development and environmental stewardship.

Evaluate

Measure outcomes, assess impact, and learn from experience to ensure transparency, accountability, and continuous improvement.

Evolve

Scale successful models, embrace innovation, and adapt to emerging challenges for lasting and sustainable impact.
